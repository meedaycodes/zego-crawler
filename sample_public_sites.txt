Public sites that allow crawlers, useful for trying domain-crawler
against something other than the bundled demo site.

1. http://books.toscrape.com/
   A fictional online bookstore, ~1000 pages across 50 categories.
   Large enough to give --concurrency a visible effect.

2. http://quotes.toscrape.com/
   A small quotes site (~10 pages) from the same project as above.
   Good for a quick first run before pointing at something larger.

3. https://www.scrapethissite.com/pages/
   A small collection of standalone practice pages (countries, hockey
   stats, paginated tables) explicitly billed as a "sandbox for
   learning web scraping."
